# Crumbl reference research (from crumblcookies.com, Nov 2026)
Official data pulled from the site's embedded product/nutrition data, plus news for company facts.

## Menu model
- "6 classic cookies plus 4 new rotating flavors every week" (official order-page SEO copy).
- Boxes: 4-pack, 6-pack, 12-pack ("Box upgraded to a {count}-Pack" auto-upgrade exists), plus minis.
- ~50 flavors in the current public flavor catalog; many partner flavors (OREO, Biscoff, REESE'S, TWIX...).

## Allergen policy (official copy)
- "Our products are made in an open kitchen that uses milk, egg, wheat, soy, tree nuts, and peanuts. Cross contamination is a possibility."
- "Any of our products may contain allergens including peanuts, tree nuts, milk, eggs, wheat, soy, and sesame."
- Tracks per-nut allergens: almond, brazil nut, cashew, coconut, hazelnut, macadamia, pecan, pine nut, pistachio.
- "Chilled cookies should be consumed or refrigerated within 2 hours."

## Fulfillment (official UI copy)
- Methods: in-store pickup, curbside ("Tap 'I'm Here'"), Crumbl Delivery, **DoorDash Delivery**, **Uber Eats Delivery**, national shipping, catering (50+ cookies, carry-out or delivery).
- Out of range: "Looks like you're a little far. Try a different address, or choose pickup instead." / "May we ship you some cookies?"
- Address check: "This address could cause delivery issues. Please check your street name and number."
- Far-away guard: confirms delivery address/pickup store when far from the user.
- Tips: "100% of tips go to the bakers / driver". Gift note on box. Box design choice.
- Catering: min quantity per flavor, increments per flavor, call store for custom flavors.
- Loyalty "Crumbs", multipliers (2x Crumbs), "Taste Weekly" subscriptions, survey bonus.

## Company (news, Oct 2026)
- Founded 2017, Logan UT (Jason McGowan, Sawyer Hemsley; both stepped down in 2026).
- 100% franchised; 1,101 stores at start of 2026, ~1,019 active on the site now.
- Sales sharply down in 2026; dozens of closures; HQ layoffs.

## UX ideas worth borrowing for KUKI's chat
1. Out-of-range → offer pickup instead (never a dead end).
2. Address sanity check before quoting.
3. Calories per whole cookie (Crumbl got criticized for "per 1/4 cookie" serving labels).
4. Allergen line on every flavor card + shared-kitchen disclaimer.
5. Gift note on the box as a chat step.
